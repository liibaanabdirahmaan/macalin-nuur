Educational Vision

Teaching Philosophy

"Science is not merely about memorizing periodic tables or anatomical structures; it is about awakening curiosity and teaching students how the natural world operates through empirical evidence and critical thought."

As an alumnus of Mogadishu University, my goal is to bridge theoretical concepts with real-world laboratory practices, empowering the next generation of Somali scientists and medical professionals.
